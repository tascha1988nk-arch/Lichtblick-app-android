How long does it take to break free from a narcissist? 💜

With help, healing can begin step by step. Without help, it often takes far too long. The self-doubt, the guilt, the fear of trusting again. And many of us know: Narcissistic abuse often leaves you with nothing, not even the money for therapy.

That's why I'm supporting Malva, a non-profit being founded in Germany that pays for therapy, counselling and coaching for people who can't afford it.

🗓 On December 1st, Giving Tuesday, I'm raising money for Malva.
🎯 Our goal: the first therapy sessions for people who need them.
🎁 Every donor gets an exclusive thank-you song from me.
